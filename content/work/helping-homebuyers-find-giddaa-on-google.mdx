[Giddaa](https://giddaa.com) helps Nigerians find and buy homes through mortgages and payment plans. When I joined, we began a gradual effort to help more people find those homes through Google.

My teammates contributed substantially throughout. As Lead Frontend Developer, I worked on making the property pages easier to discover and organising them around what homebuyers were looking for.

## Where we started

People could browse homes on Giddaa, but Google had difficulty reading the listings. Having a home on the website did not necessarily mean someone searching for it could find it.

We wanted searches for a location, a type of home or a budget to lead people to relevant properties. Getting there took several rounds of work, beginning with making the existing listings easier for Google to find.

## Help Google find and understand the homes

We rebuilt how the website presented its listings so Google could read the property details more easily. We also made sure it could find homes beyond the first page of results.

Over time, we added a directory of the site's pages for search engines to follow. We improved page titles and descriptions, and organised details such as location, price and payment options so Google could better understand what each page offered.

Each improvement built on the previous work. Search visibility grew gradually as we made more of the site accessible and useful.

## Make the links easier to understand

We changed page addresses to better describe where they lead. For example, the general property page moved from `/property` to `/houses-for-sale`, and `/plans` became `/housing-purchase-plans`.

Individual home links became more descriptive too. An address that previously ended in a long string of letters and numbers could now include the property's name or type, such as `/houses-for-sale/8-bedroom-fully-detached-triplex-29a34`.

Changing those addresses meant taking care of the old links. I set up **1,584 old addresses** to send visitors automatically to their replacements, so a saved link or a message shared earlier could still lead to the right page.

## Match the way people search

Someone looking for a home often has a location and budget in mind. We created pages that bring those choices together, so a person can arrive at a relevant selection of homes directly from Google.

For example, a search for houses in Lagos around ₦50 million can lead to [homes in Lagos between ₦50 million and ₦100 million](https://giddaa.com/browse/locations/lagos/price/50M-100M). The page gives the buyer a starting point that already reflects their budget and preferred location.

We added similar ways to browse by bedrooms, house type and payment options. I made these pages share the same underlying setup, so we could keep their titles, links and property selections consistent as the site grew.

Pages without available homes are kept out of search results. We also avoided having several versions of the same page compete for attention.

## Where the work has taken us

We have seen Giddaa appear on the first page of Google for property-for-sale searches in Nigeria. We reached those organic search positions without running ads. The progress came gradually through sustained work by the team.

Homebuyers now have more ways to reach a relevant selection of properties, including pages built around their location and budget. Clearer links also give them a better idea of what they are opening or sharing.
